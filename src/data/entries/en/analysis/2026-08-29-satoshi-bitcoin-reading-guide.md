Who Satoshi Nakamoto really is remains unknown. [Newsweek](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2014-03-06-newsweek-dorian-nakamoto/) named the wrong man, [HBO](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2024-10-08-hbo-money-electric-peter-todd/) made a documentary about it, [The New York Times](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2026-04-08-nyt-carreyrou-adam-back-satoshi-investigation/) ran its own investigation, and [a UK court](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2024-03-14-copa-v-wright-ruling/) ruled that Craig Wright had forged evidence on a grand scale to support his claim. Even [the US government has been hit with a freedom-of-information request](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2025-04-07-murphy-v-dhs-foia-lawsuit/) over it. None of that has settled anything. What follows is where the record continues.

## Satoshi and identity

<!-- faq -->
### Who is Satoshi Nakamoto?
Twelve named candidates have a documented case in this archive; none is confirmed.
**Start here:** [12 candidates compared](/BitcoinArchive/entries/analysis/2008-10-31-satoshi-identity-hypotheses-overview/)

<!-- faq -->
### Was Satoshi Nakamoto Japanese?
Satoshi's own self-claim is one unverifiable profile field, and the language and timestamp evidence argue against it — contemporaries assumed Japanese identity from the pseudonym itself, not from any other evidence.
**Start here:** [The profile, the language, the clock](/BitcoinArchive/entries/analysis/2008-10-31-satoshi-nationality-question/)

<!-- faq -->
### Did Craig Wright prove he was Satoshi?
A UK court found the opposite: that he forged evidence on a grand scale to support the claim.
**Start here:** [COPA v Wright ruling](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2024-03-14-copa-v-wright-ruling/)
**Also read:** [The case against the claim](/BitcoinArchive/entries/analysis/2016-05-02-craig-wright-satoshi-identity-hypothesis/)

<!-- faq -->
### Could Satoshi have been more than one person?
One documentary names two collaborators dividing the work, and a separate federal FOIA suit targets a 2019 government claim of four; other researchers dispute the documentary's timing.
**Start here:** [Finding Satoshi: the two-person theory](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2026-04-22-finding-satoshi-finney-sassaman-documentary/)
**Also read:** [The Murphy v DHS FOIA suit](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2025-04-07-murphy-v-dhs-foia-lawsuit/)

<!-- faq -->
### Why did Satoshi disappear?
The record shows when, not why — his earliest withdrawal signal predates the events near his final messages by seven months, read as coincidence rather than cause.
**Start here:** [Biography, Editorial readings](/BitcoinArchive/participants/satoshi-nakamoto/)

## Satoshi's holdings and activity

<!-- faq -->
### How much Bitcoin does Satoshi hold?
On-chain analysis estimates roughly 1.1 million BTC across early-mined blocks.
**Start here:** [Holdings analysis](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2021-02-08-satoshi-bitcoin-holdings-analysis/)

<!-- faq -->
### Has any of Satoshi's Bitcoin ever moved?
Almost none. Independent analyses (Lerner, Whale Alert, Lopp) each find a small spent fraction: roughly 900–1,000 BTC, or about 0.09% of Satoshi's mined blocks, with the rest untouched since mining.
**Start here:** [Holdings analysis](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2021-02-08-satoshi-bitcoin-holdings-analysis/)

<!-- faq -->
### Who received the first Bitcoin transaction?
Hal Finney, who received 10 BTC from Satoshi in Block 170 — the first person-to-person transfer.
**Start here:** [The first transaction](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2009-01-12-first-bitcoin-transaction/)

## Bitcoin's design

<!-- faq -->
### Why 21 million Bitcoin?
An "educated guess," tuned to the halving math and to amounts that would feel neither too scarce nor too abundant.
**Start here:** [21 million vs. adjustable money](/BitcoinArchive/entries/analysis/2008-10-31-fixed-supply-vs-adjustable-money/)

<!-- faq -->
### Will quantum computers break Bitcoin?
Not the ones that exist today. A migration proposal exists (BIP 360), but it's still a draft, covers only part of the threat, and has no developer consensus yet.
**Start here:** [The quantum threat](/BitcoinArchive/entries/analysis/2026-05-18-bitcoin-quantum-threat/)

## Early people and transactions

<!-- faq -->
### Who bought Bitcoin first?
NewLibertyStandard, who paid Martti Malmi $5.02 for 5,050 BTC in October 2009.
**Start here:** [First sale for fiat](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2009-10-12-martti-malmi-first-btc-sale/)

<!-- faq -->
### What happened to the guy who bought pizza with Bitcoin?
Laszlo Hanyecz, a Florida-based software developer, has no regrets — in February 2018 he bought pizza again over the Lightning Network to demonstrate Bitcoin's layer-2 scaling technology.
**Start here:** [Bitcoin Pizza Day](/BitcoinArchive/entries/aftermath/2010-05-22-bitcoin-pizza-day/)
**Also read:** [Hanyecz's later years](/BitcoinArchive/participants/laszlo-hanyecz/)

## Early history and incidents

<!-- faq -->
### What was Bitcoin's biggest bug?
On August 15, 2010, a single transaction minted 184 billion BTC. Satoshi shipped a patch within about five hours, but the network didn't fully reverse the transaction until the corrected chain overtook the bad one roughly 15 hours after the incident.
**Start here:** [184 billion BTC](/BitcoinArchive/entries/analysis/2010-08-15-overflow-incident-structure-and-paradox/)
